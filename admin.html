Administrator safety

Deleting a product is permanent. The backend should refuse deletion if order items refer to that product. Archive or unpublish such products instead. External image/video files are not automatically removed by deleting a database record.

Never share a session token or place one in a URL. If your session expires, sign in again. Do not use destructive actions as tests.
